# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment. We welcome contributors of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainer (`dinhlongviolin1` on GitHub). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1 is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
